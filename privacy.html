This policy explains what we collect when you enquire or book a viewing, and how we keep it safe.

Ask a question

Who we are

Skyline Residences is a residential development on Brickell Bay Drive in Miami. In this policy, we, us and our refer to the developer of Skyline Residences and its sales gallery.

What we collect

When you request a viewing, ask for floor plans or contact the sales gallery, we collect your name, email address, phone number and any details you choose to share about the home you are looking for.

How we use it

We use your information to arrange viewings, send the plans and prices you ask for and answer your questions. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the people who help us run the sales gallery, such as our sales team and the providers of our website and email tools, and only for the purposes above.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop sending you updates.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to viewings@skylineresidences.com.
